## Licence basis

The official Pexels licence permits free use on websites and in marketing, with modification permitted and attribution optional. It prohibits implied endorsement by pictured people or brands, stock-library redistribution, selling unaltered copies, and use of photographs as trademarks. Each selected Pexels photo page was individually checked and marked free.

The selected Unsplash image page explicitly identifies the free Unsplash License, rather than Unsplash+. That licence allows free commercial and non-commercial use, download, modification and distribution. The image is used as ordinary illustrative website photography. Optional attribution is retained here.

No paid assets, accounts or subscriptions were used. A pressure-washing candidate was rejected after visual review and removed from the deployment directory.
